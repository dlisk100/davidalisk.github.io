What happens before release

Public annual releases become checked derivatives, not a live logistics database. The nominal 2025 edition retains actual source years from 2022–2025.

  • FAF already contains published freight estimates. The atlas additionally allocates demand within freight regions and assigns road, rail and pipeline paths.
  • Water uses observed 2023 link passages scaled by 2023/2025 lock ratios. Customs trade, airport handling and production statistics each keep their own reporting scope.
  • Compact facts, geometry, admission dependencies, coarse and fine tiles, the Explore catalog and a versioned metric registry are generated before deployment.

What happens on your device

Vercel serves static files. The browser inflates compressed data and completes real preparation before enabling entry. No server calculates routes or infers freight flows for a visitor.

  • The camera controls continuous view admission, geometry detail and label placement; annual quantities remain unchanged.
  • The main thread draws networks and places on Canvas, with SVG geography and labels. During a gesture, the existing Canvas bitmap and labels transform; after settling, the unchanged exact renderer redraws.
  • Fine detail can arrive later, with loading and retry states. Idle prefetch caches compressed bytes only, rather than changing the visible network.

What a click actually means

A line click identifies the local drawn source interval, not a tracked shipment or every stretch of a named highway. A small marker and card explain the quantity, source year and geographic scope without moving the camera.

  • Observed or reported handling, published estimates and additional atlas modeling are not interchangeable. Missing values are not observed zero.
  • Saved views live on this device; share links encode camera, unit, visible layers and stable selection, not new data. Methods and Data preserve a safe return to that view.
  • Successive link passages, gateway handling and overlapping production areas cannot be summed into one national total.
